# Research Use Agreement — Child-Safety Alignment Dataset

This agreement governs access to the Child-Safety Alignment Dataset ("the Dataset") released alongside the paper *Mind the Alignment Gap* (Fikeis & Kordík, WOAH 2026). Access is granted to individual, named applicants after manual review. By requesting and accepting access, you ("the Recipient") agree to the following terms.

## 1. Content warning

The Dataset contains synthetic text depicting harmful and manipulative language directed at minors, including grooming dynamics, emotional manipulation, encouragement of risky behaviour, cyberbullying, and self-harm-adjacent material. No sexually explicit content depicting minors is included. Examples describing dangerous acts are redacted of operational detail.

## 2. Permitted use

The Recipient may use the Dataset solely for:

- research on the detection, measurement, or mitigation of harms to minors in interactions with AI systems;
- training, fine-tuning, and evaluation of **protective classifiers** and moderation systems;
- benchmarking and reproduction of the results reported in the accompanying paper.

## 3. Prohibited use

The Recipient must not:

1. use the Dataset to train, fine-tune, align, or steer any **generative** model, or to improve the capability of any system to produce harmful content;
2. use the Dataset, or any model derived from it, to produce, solicit, or distribute content harmful to minors;
3. redistribute, publish, sublicense, or otherwise make available the Dataset or any subset, derived subset, or reconstruction of it, to any third party; third parties must request access directly;
4. augment, link, or combine the Dataset with real interactions involving minors, or with any personal data of minors;
5. attempt to identify any real individual from the Dataset (all content is synthetic; no real individuals are represented);
6. use the Dataset for any commercial product or service without separate written permission from the authors.

## 4. Handling and security

The Recipient will store the Dataset on access-controlled systems, limit access to named collaborators who have themselves accepted these terms, and delete local copies when the research purpose has been fulfilled.

## 5. Derived models

Models trained on the Dataset may be released only as **classifiers** (not as generators), must carry these use restrictions forward in their documentation, and must comply with the licences of their base models.

## 6. Attribution

Any publication, preprint, or public artefact making use of the Dataset must cite the accompanying paper.

## 7. Warranty and liability

The Dataset is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Labels are machine-generated and not human-validated; safety boundaries are culturally dependent. The authors and their institutions accept no liability arising from use of the Dataset or of any system derived from it.

## 8. Termination

The authors may revoke access at any time, without prior notice, including for suspected breach of these terms. On revocation the Recipient will delete all copies of the Dataset.
